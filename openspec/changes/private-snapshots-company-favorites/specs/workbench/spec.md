# Requirements

## Requirement: 私人快照
The system SHALL retain product, direction, exchange, source and unknown fields independently for each observed position. Screenshot investment amount SHALL NOT create quantity, nominal exposure or a trade date. Snapshots and incomplete fill evidence SHALL NOT enter trade replay or returns.

## Requirement: 公司篩選與收藏
Search, industry, listing market and favorites SHALL compose. Unknown classification SHALL remain explicit. Listing market SHALL be separate from headquarters. Favorites SHALL persist under existing owner scope, full-state CAS, history and backup.

## Requirement: 保全
Full backup SHALL preserve optional snapshot and favorite state. Restore preview SHALL bind the revision and identity it inspected. Personal image bytes and payloads SHALL stay outside source/static assets.
